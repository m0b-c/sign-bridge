Upload short, consented ISL videos for individual signs. The app can play matching clips for words in a typed message. This is a clip library, not an AI-generated sign translation.

Important: This is a learning and research prototype, not a certified interpreter or a dependable full ISL sentence translator. ISL has its own grammar, regional variation, facial expressions and body movements. A hand-landmark model alone cannot capture all of that. Test with Deaf/native ISL signers and never use it for emergency, legal, medical or other critical communication. Camera processing happens in the browser; captured landmark samples are saved on this server.
